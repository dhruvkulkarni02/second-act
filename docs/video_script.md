# Demo video: script and shot list (under 2:00)

The structure follows PRD section 16. Record the **live demo** (https://second-act.bluecliff-fc92b3c6.swedencentral.azurecontainerapps.io) at **1920 × 1080**, with browser zoom at 100% and the bookmarks bar hidden. The voiceover is about 270 words, which fits two minutes at a calm pace. Use no music, or royalty-free music only, and show no real clubs or crests.

## Before you record

- Do one full dry run of the replay, so you know when each cue appears.
- Settings for the first take: Match **Harbour Town v Kestrel Athletic**, Mode **Analyst**, Language **English**, Club **None**, Speed **120×**. At 120× a half lasts about 24 seconds.
- The replay pauses at half-time (about 15 s) for the briefing. **Continue to second half** ends the pause. Speed can change mid-replay: raise it to **300×** to skip quiet stretches, and drop back to **60×** before the moments below.
- Moments to catch (match clock; the live demo serves the same verified briefing every time, so these are fixed):

| Clock | What appears |

| 15:00 | Live cue "Kestrel winning the ball higher" |
| 30:00 | Live cue "Harbour overload down the left" (the planted story) |
| 34' | Harbour goal (toast) |
